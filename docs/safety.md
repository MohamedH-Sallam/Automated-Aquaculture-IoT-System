# Safety considerations

The original report records the following construction precautions:

* Keep water and other liquids away from Arduino electronics.
* Check every electrical connection twice before connecting power.
* Insulate high voltage wiring.
* Wear heat insulating gloves when using a wax gun during assembly.

Additional operating constraints follow directly from the documented build:

* Do not remove a glass aquarium heater from water while it is energized. Allow it to cool and follow the heater manufacturer instructions before handling.
* Keep mains or heater wiring physically separated from water and low voltage control electronics.
* Use an appropriate relay, enclosure, strain relief, and residual current protection for any real water adjacent electrical installation. These are engineering safety recommendations, not features verified in the prototype report.
* Handle potassium hydroxide solution with suitable eye and skin protection. Do not dose live aquaculture water without a controlled procedure, verified concentration, and a safe upper limit.
* Confirm sensor calibration and test actuators in a non living test tank before using the system with fish.

This documentation is not a substitute for a formal electrical, chemical, or animal welfare risk assessment.
